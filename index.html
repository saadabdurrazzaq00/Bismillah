A collection for daily remembrance

Begin with remembrance.

Seventeen supplications to seek Allah's forgiveness, praise Him, ask for protection, and seek blessed provision. Read the Arabic, understand the meaning, and listen at your own pace.

Audio is played from locally hosted recordings. Narrated repetition counts are distinct from personal preferences. A count of one can describe an occasion of recitation rather than a religiously mandated numerical limit. Some entries are personal supplications rather than established Prophetic wording.
